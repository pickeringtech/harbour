# Code of Conduct

Harbour follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We pledge to make participation harassment-free regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

Be respectful, assume good intent while addressing concrete impact, accept
constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment,
sexualised attention, personal attacks, trolling, and publishing private
information are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers. Reports
will be reviewed promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may correct, warn,
temporarily ban, or permanently ban behavior according to its impact and
pattern. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing Harbour.
